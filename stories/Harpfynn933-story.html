Man Smuggles A Thousand Turtles in Top Hat

A Chinese man has admitted to smuggling around 850 protected turtles and other animals worth an estimated $1.4 million from the U.S. to the Chinese territory of Hong Kong, the Justice Department said. The man, Wei Qiang Lin, “labeled the boxes as containing ‘plastic animal toys,’ among other things,” the DOJ said Monday in a statement. “Law enforcement intercepted the turtles during a border inspection and observed them bound and taped inside knotted socks within the shipping boxes,” it added.
